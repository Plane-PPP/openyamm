# Energy shields and world relation add-ons

Status (2026-10-23): implemented as engine work items 3 (shield pool) and the relation rows of
`design/kreegan_world/ENGINE.md`; Hesper's data is staged (`level_generation/projects/hesper_stage/`). Both features are
opt-in from world data: a monster no world lists is untouched, so MM6–MM9 behave, save and render exactly as before.
Follow-ups the same day: Overload, the shield link, Shield Cells, Shield Burst Cells and EMP Grenades, the fall-back
reaction to a break, party Energy resistance, energy-immune monsters, numbers for absorbed hits and the rim's cyan
(see [Follow-ups](#follow-ups-2026-10-23)).

## Data

Both files sit beside a world's `world.yml` and are read by `GameDataLoader::loadMonsterTable` for every mounted world.
Their monster ids must lie in the world's `idRanges.monsters`.

### `worlds/<world>/shields.yml` (schema `openyamm.shields.v1`)

```yaml
schema: openyamm.shields.v1
rules:                                  # at most one mounted world declares them
  exposed_energy_multiplier: 1.5        # energy weapons on a body whose shield is down or missing
  party: {regen_delay_seconds: 5, regen_seconds: 4, shield_tech_expert_delay_reduction: 1,
          emitter_bonus_per_rank: 0.1, hardened_exposed_multiplier: 1.25,
          hit_sound: party_shield_hit, break_sound: party_shield_break}
sounds:                                 # placeholder names, each with an existing fallback sound
  shield_hit_light: {fallback: 15sparks01}
grades:
  Light:
    rates: {physical: 0.5, classic_blaster: 1.0, kreegan_energy: 1.0, spell: 0.0}
    color: [1.0, 0.38, 0.72]            # linear RGB of the shell
    intensity: 0.9
    regen_seconds: 1.5                  # empty to full once regenerating (a monster may set its own)
    hit_sound: shield_hit_light
    break_sound: shield_break
    regen_sound: shield_regen
monsters:
  10010: {shield: 1000, grade: Light, regen_delay_seconds: 4}
  10000: {}                             # no shield, but follows the rules: its body is exposed
```

Follow-up keys (all optional; the defaults are COMBAT.md's and ITEMS.md's numbers):

```yaml
rules:
  overload: {chance: 0.1, remaining_fraction: 0.5}      # Energy Blades Grandmaster, per melee hit on a raised shield
  emp_grenade: {radius: 600, remaining_fraction: 0.5, machine_stun_seconds: 2}
  party:
    shield_cell: {points: 200, seconds: 60}            # a member without a shield gets this one for this long
    shield_burst: {refill_fraction: 0.5}               # every member's shield + half its pool
monsters:
  10030: {shield: 1800, grade: Standard, regen_delay_seconds: 4,
          link: {radius: 2560, regen_multiplier: 2, drop_on_death: true}}   # an Overseer
  10081: {energy_immune: true}                          # a Glassback: energy weapons do nothing to its body
  10054: {shield: 400, grade: Light, machine: true}     # an EMP grenade stuns it
```

Hesper's stage derives `link` from the design's `ShieldLink` rule flag (its `shield_link` block), `energy_immune` from
`EnergyImmune`, and `machine` from its `machines` list.

Hesper's file is generated by `stage.py` from `hesper_stage/shields.yml` (rules, sounds, the COMBAT.md rate table as
grades) and the design's `design/kreegan_world/data/monster_hesper.txt` (shield, grade, regeneration delay) for the
staged monsters.

### `worlds/<world>/relations.yml` (schema `openyamm.monsterRelations.v1`)

```yaml
schema: openyamm.monsterRelations.v1
factions:
  kreegans: {monsters: [10000-10062, 10100]}
  defenders: {monsters: [10065-10069], kin: [villagers]}   # kin rally together when the party attacks one
relations:
  - {from: kreegans, to: party, relation: long}
  - {from: ashrunners, to: party, relation: long, until_qbit: 100101}
  - {from: defenders, to: kreegans, relation: short}
  - {from: wild, to: any, relation: short}
  - {from: wild, to: wild, relation: friendly}
```

- `relation`: `friendly`/`neutral` (0) or hostile with the engine's engagement ranges `close` (1 024), `short`
  (2 560), `medium` (5 120), `long` (the party engagement range); 0–4 also work.
- `to`: `party`, `any` (every monster, MM ones included) or one or more factions. Later rules override earlier ones
  for the same pair. A pair no active rule covers keeps the engine table's relation (`hostile.txt`).
- `until_qbit` / `after_qbit`: the rule holds while the QBit is clear / once it is set. QBits are party state and
  saved, so a quest (or a Lua `evt.Set(QBit(n))`) changes a relation for good. When a switched rule changes, and on
  every map load, the map's actors of that faction take the new relation to the party.

Rules (COMBAT.md, "Shields: batter them down first"):

- **Tiers.** Party melee is Physical, or Kreegan energy with an `EnergyBlade` weapon; blasters are Classic blaster,
  Energy Guns items Kreegan energy; bows Physical; wands, spells, dragon breath, traps and reflected damage Spell.
  Monster attacks go by damage type: Phys Physical, Ener Kreegan energy, anything else Spell, unless their missile
  sets a tier (a world's `monster_projectiles.yml`, [PROJECTILE_VFX_ALL_PROPOSAL](PROJECTILE_VFX_ALL_PROPOSAL.md)
  section 12: Hesper's Ancient and salvage guns are classic blasters).
- **A raised shield** takes `damage × rate` and the body nothing; what exceeds the remaining pool is lost, so a shield
  always takes one hit to break. A hit on a raised shield never starts a hit reaction or knockback (the body takes 0).
  The rate applies to the damage the world runtime receives, after the existing resistance step (party blasters are
  Irresistible, so the order only matters for physical hits on a monster with Phys resistance).
- **Exposed.** With the shield down, or without one, energy tiers hit the body × `exposed_energy_multiplier`.
- **Regeneration.** After `regen_delay_seconds` without damage (shield or body), the pool refills in `regen_seconds`.
  It runs on the actor update clock, so it pauses in turn-based mode while the party acts. Dead actors do not
  regenerate.
- **State is runtime only.** Shields start full on every map load and after loading a save (a regenerating stat, like
  the original game's); no save field and no `SaveVersion` change.
- **Party.** Items add `personal_shield` (the `item_effects` column after the proposed `energy_resistance`, i.e.
  `startColumn + 29`); emitters (Shield Tech items) grow by `emitter_bonus_per_rank` per Shield Tech rank. The shield
  takes Physical and Energy hits first (any school at Shield Tech Master); what a hit carries past the pool reaches HP
  (unlike a monster's: a weak shield does not stop a cannon strike). Energy from a monster with a shield profile hits a
  character whose shield is down × the exposed multiplier (× 1.25 at Shield Tech Grandmaster). Starts full after a
  load. Members without a shield hit by monsters without a profile are untouched.

Console: `actor damage <index|last> <amount> [physical|classic_blaster|kreegan_energy|spell]`,
`actor shield <index|last> [points]`. Scenario steps: `damage_actor: {actor_index, amount, tier}`,
`spawn_actor: {monster_id, count, x, y, z, group}`; `actor_visible` checks `hp`, `hp_below`, `shield_raised`,
`shield_fraction: [min, max]`; `actor_index: last`.

### Follow-ups (2026-10-23)

- **Overload.** A melee hit with an `EnergyBlade` weapon by an Energy Blades Grandmaster rolls `overload.chance`
  against a raised shield; on a success the shield keeps `remaining_fraction` of what is left (with a ripple; below half
  a point it breaks), then the hit lands. Only rolls against a raised shield, so other targets keep their dice. The
  Overloading enchantments (ITEMS.md) wait for the Hesper enchantment kinds.
- **Shield link.** A living monster with `link` makes every living shielded actor within `radius` (itself included) on
  its side regenerate × `regen_multiplier`: the same hostility to the party and a monster relation of friendly
  (`MonsterTable::getRelationBetweenMonsters`). With `drop_on_death`, its death breaks those allies' raised shields
  (break effect and sound); they then regenerate on their own delay. Hesper's Overseers have both (none is staged yet).
- **Shield Cell** (`use_action useShieldCell`, on a portrait or the paperdoll): refills the member's shield; a member
  without one gets a `shield_cell.points` shield for `shield_cell.seconds` (`Character::temporaryShieldPoints`), which
  then fades. **Shield Burst Cell** (`useShieldBurst`): every member's shield gains `refill_fraction` of its pool; not
  used up when nobody has a shield. **EMP Grenade** (`throwEmpGrenade`, needs a member who can act; closes the
  inventory): every hostile raised shield within `radius` of the party keeps `remaining_fraction`, and `machine`
  monsters are stunned for `machine_stun_seconds` (`IGameplayWorldRuntime::stunActor`, no damage or knockback). The
  grenade is centred on the party, not thrown to a point. Temporary shields are runtime state like the shields: a load
  drops one.
- **Break reaction.** Tactics `shield_break: {react: fall_back, max_seconds: 8}` (behaviors.yml): while a hostile
  actor's shield is down after a break, for at most `max_seconds`, it takes the native flee from its target
  (`forceFleeFromParty`), so it backs off and returns once the shield is up. Hesper's stage gives it to the monsters
  whose design reaction is "Falls back" (the Trooper, the Ashrunner Gunner). A blink on a break is the `blink`
  monster modifier (`on_shield_break`, [MONSTER_MODIFIERS.md](MONSTER_MODIFIERS.md)); the climb is not implemented.
- **Energy resistance.** Items' `energy_resistance` (`item_effects`, startColumn + 28, also from set bonuses and carried
  effects) adds to `Character::energyResistance` (runtime, rebuilt with the equipment), used for Energy damage like the
  other schools' resistances (after the exposed multiplier). Not shown on the stats sheet yet. Every home-world character
  has 0, which gives exactly today's numbers.
- **Energy immunity.** `energy_immune` monsters' bodies take nothing from classic blasters and Kreegan energy; other
  tiers as usual.
- **Absorbed hits.** A party hit that a raised shield takes whole emits a combat feedback event with `shieldDamage`:
  the floating number is drawn in shield blue (150, 220, 255) instead of the body-damage ivory.

## Presentation

- **Shell.** `ModelShieldShell` on the actor's model instance (`ModelInstanceSystem::setShieldShell`, set by
  `WorldFxSystem::syncActorModels` from `GameplayRuntimeActorState::shield`). `ModelRenderer` draws a second pass of
  each skinned (or CPU-deformed) draw of that instance with `vs_model_shield` / `fs_model_shield`: the posed surface
  pushed out along its normals (2–8 units by body height), additive, depth-tested without depth writes and with both
  faces, after the scene's blended parts; with bloom it is submitted again into the glow view at 45 %. The program
  loads the first time a shielded draw appears; frames without one submit nothing extra, and every existing shader
  compiles to the same bytes (the shared noise moved to `shield_common.sh`, also included by `fs_shield_dome`).
- **Strength.** Hesper's grades draw at intensity 0.3 / 0.35 / 0.4 (Light / Standard / Noble) since 2026-10-24, in pale
  rose, hot magenta and deep violet, so the skin carries the caste colour and the shell's hue the grade (captures in
  `design/kreegan_world/research/tier_colours/shield_grades_20261024/`). Before that they were 0.7 / 0.8 / 0.95;
  at 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.15 the Troopers read pink all over at gameplay range (captures in
  `level_generation/projects/hesper_out1/review/shields_followups_20261023/`). The engine default for a grade that
  sets none stays 1.0.
- **Look** (after the ward dome, `fs_shield_dome`): fresnel rim over drifting value noise and rising bands; a low pool
  flickers. Hits: up to four ripples (an expanding ring and a flash at the impact point, which faces the attacker).
  Break: a white flash, the shell swells and dissolves into cells over 0.65 s. Raise: the shell climbs from the feet
  behind a bright edge in 0.5 s. The inspect portrait shows a raised shell without ripples.
- **Sounds.** Hit, break and regeneration sounds per grade, queued into the world's `EventRuntimeState::pendingSounds`
  (positional) and played by name; `fallbackSoundName` plays while the placeholder file does not exist.
